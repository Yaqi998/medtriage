Sample chest X-rays

Images to try in the Medical Image Triage app.

How to try them

  1. Save an image. Tap or click Download. On a phone you can also long-press the image and choose Save to Photos or Download image.
  2. Open the app at yaqi998.github.io/medtriage.
  3. Upload it in step 2 and compare the app's answer with the true label shown here.

These 10 images were drawn at random from the held-out test set (patients the model never saw during training), one image per patient. They were resized to at most 1024 px.

Images: Kermany, Zhang & Goldbaum (2018), Mendeley Data, licensed CC BY 4.0; resized from the originals. Paediatric patients aged 1–5. Educational demo, not a medical device.
