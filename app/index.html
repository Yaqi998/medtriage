Model: ResNet18 fine-tuned on chest X-rays from Kermany et al. 2018 (Mendeley Data, CC BY 4.0); sample images are from the same dataset. Inference runs locally with ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). Nothing is uploaded anywhere. After the first visit the app is stored on your device and works offline; on a phone you can add it to your home screen.
